## LinkedIn company page post

99.7% of Denver's active short-term rental licenses are their holder's only license.

The register pulled October 19, 2026 holds 2,338 records, of which 2,083 carry an active status. Those 2,083 licenses sit under 2,080 licensee names: 2,077 licensees hold exactly one, 3 hold two, and none holds three or more. The top 10 holders together account for 13 licenses (0.62%). Not one active license is filed under a name carrying a business-entity marker such as an LLC, a corporation, or a trust.

Today's brief reads the full register by holder, parcel, and neighborhood. Concentration shows up in two other places, not in holders: 12 parcels hold 84 active licenses between them (each license under a different name - multi-unit buildings licensed unit by unit, the largest parcel carrying 32), and neighborhood density runs from zero in three neighborhoods to 27.1 licenses per 1,000 housing units in Whittier, against a citywide rate of 5.8. The licensed footprint also tracks recent construction: across 75 neighborhoods, the rank correlation between STR density and new-building permits per 1,000 housing units is 0.64.
